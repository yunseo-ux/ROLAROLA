ABOUT

ROLAROLA EMBRACES THE SPIRIT OF THE NOUVELLE VAGUE — ROMANTIC, SENSITIVE, AND QUIETLY FREE.

Rolarola is inspired by the essence of the nouvelle vague.
Against convention yet deeply romantic and sensitive, the brand expresses a unique French mood with a soft and effortless charm.

Through its distinctive sensibility and everyday elegance, rolarola shares values of individuality, joy, and emotional ease. It offers not only style, but also a feeling of rest, comfort, and gentle healing for women.
